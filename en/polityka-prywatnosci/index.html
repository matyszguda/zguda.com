Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Controller and contact

The data controller is Mateusz Zguda, who runs zguda.com. For privacy enquiries, please use the contact details on the website.

Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited. I analyse visits to public pages, traffic sources and information about browsers, devices and approximate locations to improve the website. Measurement uses cookie identifiers and technical connection data. According to Google, IP addresses in GA4 are used to determine location and are not stored in full.

The _ga and _ga_787SY0YXF7 cookies are set to expire after 180 days. Google Signals and advertising personalisation are disabled. Page-view measurement receives URLs without query parameters or fragments.

Your choice

Analytics is enabled by default. You can turn it off here. I will remember your choice for 180 days.

Browser settings

The website stores your selected language in the browser, and the style you select with the washing machine for the current tab. You can delete cookies and website data in your browser settings.

Hosting, fonts and data recipients

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are provided by Google Fonts. These providers receive the technical data needed to handle requests, including your IP address. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area under the transfer mechanisms used by those providers.

More information: Google’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and your rights

The site does not have a contact form. If you contact me by email or LinkedIn, correspondence data is processed to handle your enquiry; external services apply their own privacy policies.

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, or object to processing. The scope of these rights depends on the basis and purpose of processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of Poland’s Personal Data Protection Office.
