02 / SOCIAL ACTION

Zero Camps

SUPPORT IN 2022–2026
3,600people displaced by the war received support from the foundation
102k+nights of accommodation provided by the foundation

Each night for each person is counted separately.

I founded and run a foundation supporting people fleeing the war in Ukraine. We started by opening up an empty office in Kraków.

Between 2022 and 2026, Marina played a key role in caring for the people supported by the foundation.

About me and my role

We began with 22 places in an empty office in Kraków. Later, we rented two hostels where we accommodated 90 people in total, and were given access to flats on Lea Street.

I organised accommodation, partnerships and the foundation’s team. Over time, we also began building a financial base for our continuing work — that idea led to the Ciepło restaurant.

03 / FOOD AND COMMUNITY

Ciepło

A restaurant owned by the foundation. Ukrainian food, art and hospitality at 15 Dietla Street in Kraków. As president of the management board, I’m responsible for the business side of the venture.

About me and my role

Ciepło grew out of cooking together at the foundation’s accommodation sites. With Olena’s considerable involvement, we opened a restaurant that combines traditional Ukrainian food with a contemporary approach, art and Carpathian culture.

We want it to be a meeting place and a source of financial independence for the foundation. I’m responsible for the business side of the venture; the restaurant’s day-to-day operations are in the team’s hands.

05 — OUTSIDE WORK

On a personal note.

I spend about 70 days a year outdoors. I love mountains of every size and in every season.

Since 2013, I’ve played squash socially in winter (I’m not very good, and I don’t feel any need to improve). Sometimes I enjoy designing and making a piece of furniture or a lamp myself.

I’m interested in materials, and sometimes I dream of getting other people interested in them too. (Why aren’t you interested? It’s fascinating!) On this and related subjects, I recommend the hilarious Steve Mould channel, the delightful channel by Tom Scott, the calming channel by Sabine Hossenfelder and the relaxing and inspiring Primitive Technology, books by Mark Miodownik and titles published by Karakter.

I grew up with a curiosity about architecture and language. In later years, I broadened those interests to include design in its widest sense, interaction and service design, and the quantitative and qualitative research connected with those fields. Here, I’m lucky enough to bring my natural interests into my work.
