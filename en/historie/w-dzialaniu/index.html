PERSONAL PERSPECTIVE / MY MOTTO

It’s in the doing that the idea comes.

edmund bacon

AS TOLD BY MATEUSZ ZGUDA

In 2010, I came across a funny YouTube video on the How to Architect channel. I often return to this story by Doug Patt, in which he quotes Edmund Bacon: “It’s in the doing that the idea comes.” It is pretty much my motto for life.

Bacon was an American architect and urban planner, one of the people who shaped post-war Philadelphia. He led the city’s planning commission for more than twenty years and also wrote the book Design of Cities. He designed whole sections of the city, but this one thought also resonates with me in much smaller, everyday matters.

I understand it in a very practical way: we can start with only the outline of an idea. Something interests us, there is something we want to test, and we make a first attempt. Only then do we notice possibilities we could not see before. A sketch, a conversation or a prototype gives us something to respond to. The next step becomes easier.

I often remind myself of those words when I try to work everything out in advance. They give me the freedom to start with something imperfect and see where it takes me. After all, doing is part of coming up with ideas.
